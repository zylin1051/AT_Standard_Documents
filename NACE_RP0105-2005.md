# NACE RP0105-2005 - Liquid-Epoxy Coatings for External Repair, Rehabilitation, and Weld Joints on Buried Steel Pipelines

## Original Document
See the original document [here](NACE_RP0105-2005.pdf).

## Scope
- Recommended-practice guidance for establishing minium requirements for **liquid-epoxy coatings** applied to the **external surface of buried pipelines**:
  - External coating repair;
  - Rehabilitation of previously coated pipelines;
  - Coating of field weld joints;
  - Buried steel pipeline applications;
  - Field-applied liquid-epoxy coating systems.
- The standard addresses the following major areas:
  
| Area | Summary |
|---|---|
| Material selection | Coating material approval, supplier data, coating properties, batch control, shelf life. |
| Surface preparation | Removal of contaminants, blast cleaning, surface profile, dew point control, overlap with existing coating. |
| Coating application | Brush-grade or spray-grade epoxy, temperature control, WFT/DFT control, cure requirements. |
| Inspection and testing | Surface profile, visual inspection, curing, adhesion, hardness, DFT, holiday detection. |
| Repair | Compatible repair material, repair overlap, surface roughening, repair thickness. |
| Backfilling | Coating must be sufficiently cured to a backfill-ready condition before backfilling. | 

- Note: the function of the coating is to prevent corrosion **when used in conjunction with cathodic protection**. Therefore, NACE RP0105 should be understood as part of an external corrosion-control system, not as a replacement for cathodic protection design or CP performance verification.
- The standard recognizes that liquid-epoxy coating repair, rehabilitation, or field-joint coating can be applied:
  - In a ditch;
  - Over a ditch;
  - Beside a ditch;
  - On pipelines that are in service;
  - On pipelines that are out of service;
  - On segmented pipeline sections.
 
- Application is **dependent on project-specific conditions**, especially **temperature and field environment**.
